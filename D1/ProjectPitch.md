Brainstorm:

Motion Capture game using Move net Lightning, to simulate real time movement using a webcam or camera. This will be targeted towards Children that are in elementary school, as a cheap option for indoor recess.

Ideas:

1. A temple run type of game, where the player has to dodge out of the way of incoming objects to get a higher score. There will be 3 lanes with obstacles coming towards you on each respective lane. There will also be 4 main controls the player can do. [Move Left, Move Right, Jump, Slide] ~ [ Move your center of mass or body to the left hands can also be an option, Same thing but for right, Jump, and duck/crouch or move your hands down]
2.A Game that gives problems on a projector, which you then have to hit with a ball to get the correct answer. This will be done using a live tracking of a ball in comparison to a projector to determine where the ball is in comparison to the answer.
3.A Hub for all the game ideas, as a platform for schools to be able to choose any games they want to play whether that be a quiz or a temple run game, all for education purposes, or indoor recess. It will be open source so people can add whatever they want. of course it will be monitored, as to not cause any issues.

Pitch: 3.
I will create a HUB for teachers/kids to help make indoor recess a little less boring for schools who are not in the best of financial situations. Developers will be able to create games easily, using a motion capture called Move Net Lightning. It will be both a learning activity, and way to express creative freedom for developers. It will have a very simple design process for people to make games, much easier. However, all the games will have to make use of a pretrained Mocap, and have a focus on kids' entertainment within an educational facility. All of it will be open source, and easily downloadable so anyone can create anything, with heavy censorship restrictions on what is on the app, for safe experience for children. I will make it so each game can be downloaded off of an integrated browser for easier usage, and so things inside the games that have yet to be removed don't show up on the main screen of the app or site. It will be an extremely difficult project, and take many hours to complete, but it will hopefully create an opportunity to learn how to create games and code, along with entertaining games for kids.
